Base site

The lunar south pole

At the pole the Sun never rises more than about 1.5° above the horizon. Light arrives sideways, and long shadows shape the terrain. Every structure in the program answers to that low light.

Zoom · power

Ridges that stay lit

Some crater rims and ridges near the pole, such as the ridge between Shackleton and de Gerlache, are sunlit for most of the year. That is where the power plant goes: vertical solar masts catching horizontal light.

Zoom · resources

Craters that never see the Sun

Crater floors in permanent shadow are among the coldest places measured in the solar system, and they hold water ice. NASA's LCROSS impactor confirmed water when it struck Cabeus in 2009.

02 · The block

One family, sized to the robot

A redesign of the lab's block as an osteomorphic block (Dyskin et al. 2003, 2005): two matching curved faces, the bottom one shifted by half a length, so each course self-aligns in running bond and holds without mortar under a peripheral constraint. Grades change density, finish and seat, never the interlock.

Compression only

Regolith has almost no tensile strength. Pavers, berms, piers, arches, vaults. No mortar, no adhesive.

Self-adjusting

Concave meets convex, so a slightly misplaced block slides into place. Low-precision robots and in situ printing are enough.

Sized to the robot

Mass is set by manipulator payload and inertia, not by the structure.

Needs a constraint

The assembly holds only when confined: by a frame, the terrain, tendons or its own weight.

05 · Robot–block interface

Exploring end-effector geometry

The grip is where the block and the robot meet. The method: keep each block body and its interlock fixed, vary only the interface module, and test every interface with the end-effector designed for it.

Why gripping from above matters

Options 2 and 3 grip from above. One end-effector then works for walls, columns and plates, because the top face is free in all of them, and blocks can be placed tightly side by side with no room needed for side access. Option 1 stays as the baseline: it runs on the lab's current hardware.

How the options are organised

One Grasshopper definition, one switch

The block body and its interlocking features stay the same for all options. The grip is a separate interface module, chosen with a Value List (a drop-down). Every block in the family can then carry any of the three interfaces. The switch below drives the 3D explorer and the drawings on this page.
